## 1. Content Accuracy & Transparency

| Rule | Details | Severity |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Accurate Claims Only** | All product claims must be honest, verifiable, and not misleading. No false promises about efficacy. | Content removal + point deduction |
| **Product-Content Match** | The product shown in the video/image must match the linked TikTok Shop listing exactly. No bait-and-switch. | Content removal |
| **Commercial Disclosure** | All promotional content must use TikTok's built-in disclosure tools ("Paid partnership" / "Promotional content" tags). | Restricted reach → removal |
| **24h Disclosure Fix Window** | If flagged for missing disclosure, creators have ~24 hours to fix before reach is restricted. | Reach suppression |

---

## 2. Pricing & Discount Rules

| Rule | Details | Severity |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Price Accuracy** | Any price/discount mentioned must be accurate, clear, and currently valid. | Content removal |
| **Conditional Pricing** | If price depends on SKU, coupon, bundle, or promo period, the conditions MUST be explicitly disclosed. | Content removal |
| **Misleading Price Claims** | Suggesting a price is available to everyone when it's limited or conditional is PROHIBITED. | Account penalties |
| **Our Rule: No Direct Prices** | To stay safe, we NEVER mention specific prices in captions or dialogue. We direct viewers to "beg kuning" instead. | Self-imposed |

## 3. AI-Generated Content (AIGC) Policy

| Rule | Details | Severity |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Mandatory AIGC Label** | Any content with realistic AI-generated people, places, or events MUST be labeled using TikTok's "AI-generated content" toggle. | Content removal + suppression |
| **Synthetic Faces** | AI-generated or face-swapped individuals require disclosure. | Content removal |
| **Voice Clones** | AI-generated narration or voice impersonation requires disclosure. | Content removal |
| **AI Backgrounds** | Realistic AI-generated scenes/environments require disclosure. | Content removal |
| **Exemptions** | AI-assisted text (scripts, captions, hashtags) does NOT require disclosure. | N/A |
| **C2PA Detection** | TikTok uses Content Credentials (C2PA) to auto-detect synthetic media, even if creator doesn't self-disclose. | Auto-label or removal |
| **Deepfakes Banned** | Impersonating real people (public figures, celebrities) via AI is strictly prohibited. | Immediate removal + ban |
| **Private Individuals** | Synthetic media featuring real private individuals is entirely banned. | Immediate removal + ban |
| **Ads: Mandatory Since July 2026** | All AI-generated content in paid advertisements must carry disclosure labels. | Ad rejection + penalties |
| **AIGC Seller Playbook (Sep 2026)** | AI imagery must not alter key physical product attributes (size, color, material, cut, or safety features) in a manner that misrepresents the actual physical item. | Listing deactivation + points |
| **Our Workflow Note** | Our keyframe images and Flow AI videos are AI-generated. When posting, user MUST enable the "AI-generated content" toggle in TikTok's posting settings. | Self-imposed |

## 4. Prohibited Content Categories

| Category | Details | Severity |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Misleading Claims** | False promises about product efficacy or functionality. | Content removal + point deduction |
| **Fake Engagement** | Bots, fake stories, "guilt-tripping" tactics, sympathy-based baiting. | Account restriction |
| **Non-Interactive Content** | Static images with music, looping short clips, zero-engagement content. | Suppression + posting limits |
| **Drugs & Tobacco** | Promoting, using, or implying use of drugs, tobacco, nicotine, e-cigarettes. | Immediate removal |
| **Adult Content** | Sexually explicit content, products, or services. | Immediate removal |
| **Illegal Goods** | Stolen, counterfeit, or Malaysian-law-banned items. | Immediate removal + ban |
| **Gambling** | Content promoting or facilitating gambling. | Immediate removal |
| **Underage Content** | Minors representing or promoting products without adult supervision. | Immediate removal |
| **Weight-Loss Medical Claims** | Claims like "Nature's Ozempic", unsafe exercise promotion, exceeding dosage recommendations. | Content removal |

## 5. Engagement & Account Health

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Promotion Performance Score (PPS)** | Replaced PQP system effective **Aug 27, 2026**. Evaluates shoppable video/LIVE quality and compliance (50%) + product selection (50%). Target: **PPS >= 4.5**. If score drops below **3.0**, visibility is penalized and access to campaigns/tools restricted. Current Malaysia campaign guidance requires **PPS >= 3.5** for Affiliate Creators. |
| **Creator Health Rating (CHR)** | TikTok tracks compliance via CHR dashboard. Monitor regularly alongside PPS. Current Malaysia campaign guidance requires **CHR >= 150**. |
| **5-Video Misleading Threshold** | If 5+ "misleading" or "non-interactive" videos are posted in 7 days, posting limits cap creators to only 7 shoppable videos/week. |
| **90-Day Violation Window** | Repeated violations within 90 days → content removal, suspension, or permanent e-commerce ban. |
| **Engagement Bait Detection** | Algorithm detects and suppresses "like-for-like", fake incentives, and engagement bait. |
| **Inactive Shop Policy (Updated Sep 2026)** | Accounts inactive for 40 calendar days face chat restrictions and product listing deactivation (buyers notified of inactivity after 7 days). Sellers must process orders promptly to Ready to Ship (RTS) or actively interact via Seller Center/API. Holiday Mode must be toggled if taking a break. |
| **Customer Service Chat Assistant (Oct 1, 2026)** | Auto-enablement for shops with low response rate (<30-day review cycle) to maintain buyer satisfaction and PPS. |
| **Community Guidelines Update (Sep 24, 2026)** | Formalized criteria for bans and strict labeling for synthetic media and AI-edited promotional content. |
| **Health Claims (Updated July 2026)** | Stricter restrictions on body structure alteration claims, GLP-1/Ozempic medicine comparisons, and exceeding dosage guidelines. |
| **Appeals** | Creators can appeal removals through the app's "Content violations and bans" section. |

## 6. Hashtag & Caption Rules

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **No Spam Tags** | Generic spam hashtags (#fyp, #viral, #RacunTikTok) are flagged and can reduce reach. |
| **Accurate Descriptions** | Captions must provide comprehensive, truthful details about the product. |
| **Product Pin** | Reference the product link 3+ times per video for best conversion (platform guidance). |
| **Our Rule: No Em-Dash** | Never use `—` or `–` in captions. Use `:`, `,`, or natural phrasing. |

## 7. Our Self-Imposed Safety Rules (Stricter Than Policy)

These are rules we enforce on ourselves to maintain a clean account health:

| Rule | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| No direct price mentions | Avoids risk of "misleading price" violations due to SKU/promo variability. |
| No generic spam hashtags | Avoids algorithmic suppression and keeps content targeting high-intent audiences. |
| No em-dash characters | Some TikTok caption renderers display them incorrectly; cleaner formatting. |
| Always use "saya" (never "aku") | More polished, professional tone that builds trust with Malaysian audience. |
| No waving/raised hands in Scene 3 | Prevents awkward, unnatural body language in AI-generated videos. |
| AIGC disclosure reminder | Always remind user to enable "AI-generated content" toggle when posting. |
